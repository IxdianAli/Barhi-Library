OUR STORY

Rooted in Barhi.
Open to everyone.

Barhi Library is a community-led initiative taking shape with a simple belief: access to books and learning can help everyone grow. We’re bringing people together to build a welcoming space, grow a shared collection and make reading more accessible.

We’re still developing the project, and we’ll share updates as the library grows. Barhi Library is not yet a registered NGO.
